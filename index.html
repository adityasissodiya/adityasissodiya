Command Succession under Intermittent Connectivity: Enforcing and Reconciling Human Authority in Incident Response

In preparation

Repository: rescue-ois.

Aditya Sissodiya, Eric Chiquito, Johan Kristiansson, and Ulf Bodin

Approximately halfway to submission.

Studies command succession in rescue-service incident response over intermittent links: who may give orders when the incident commander is cut off, how disconnected devices enforce that, and how orders given in the meantime are reconciled.

EQuack: Deterministic Replay for Eventually Consistent Access Control

Under review

IEEE Internet of Things Journal, regular article.

Aditya Sissodiya, Johan Kristiansson, Eric Chiquito, and Ulf Bodin

Submitted on 3 June 2026; awaiting decision.

An access-control model for intermittently connected, multi-stakeholder systems: replicas keep operating while disconnected and, after reconnecting, converge on the same authorized state by deterministically replaying a shared log through a deny-wins authorization gate. Evaluated with a Rust prototype.

Publications

  1. Formal Verification for Preventing Misconfigured Access Policies in Kubernetes Clusters

    Aditya Sissodiya, Eric Chiquito, Ulf Bodin, and Johan Kristiansson

    IEEE Access, vol. 13, pp. 141798–141813, 2025. Published Journal article

    Models Kubernetes RBAC and admission policies in first-order logic and uses an SMT solver to search for counter-examples to security invariants before policies reach the cluster.

  2. Objective- and Utility-Based Negotiation for Access Control

    Aditya Sissodiya, Ulf Bodin, and Olov Schelén

    Proceedings of the 11th International Conference on Information Systems Security and Privacy (ICISSP 2025), vol. 2, pp. 493–501, 2025. Published Conference paper

    Proposes an algorithm that automates access-policy negotiation among stakeholders by quantifying their preferences with utility functions and using optimization to reach consensus.

Licentiate thesis

Multi-Stakeholder Access Control in Data Ecosystems

Aditya Sissodiya

Licentiate thesis, Luleå University of Technology, 2026. Published

Addresses both agreement and enforcement of access rules in multi-stakeholder data ecosystems: utility-based negotiation of a common rule set, formal verification of cloud-native access policies (shown on Kubernetes), and an outline of an offline-capable access control model for distributed ecosystems.

Supervisors: Ulf Bodin, Eric Chiquito, and Johan Kristiansson. As listed in the thesis. The current doctoral supervisory team is not stated here.
